## Spacing Scale

Declared values (all multiples of 4; use the existing `--space-*` tokens where values match):

| Token | Value | Usage |
|-------|-------|-------|
| xs | 4px | Icon/arrow gap and optical alignment |
| sm | 8px | Compact label and CTA-link gaps |
| md | 16px | Copy group and mobile page-gutter spacing |
| lg | 24px | Copy-to-action and desktop internal spacing |

| 2xl | 48px | Hero content block spacing and touch-target minimum |
| 3xl | 64px | Desktop inset and major separation |

Exceptions: none. Use a minimum 48px height for every hero link/button, including secondary links; this is a control-size requirement, not a spacing-scale exception.

## Typography

Use exactly these four role sizes and two weights in the hero. Fluid sizing may interpolate the Display role between its stated viewport bounds; do not add extra semantic type tiers.

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height |
|------|------|--------|-------------|
| Label / eyebrow | 14px | 600 | 1.3 |
| Body | 16px | 400 | 1.5 |
| Heading / lead | 24px | 600 | 1.2 |
| Display / name | clamp(40px, 6.5vw, 72px) desktop; 40px mobile | 600 | 1.0 |

Use uppercase and modest tracking for the eyebrow only. Keep the h1 and supporting sentence in sentence/title case. Limit paragraph measure to 48rem on desktop and 34rem on mobile. Do not use extra-bold 700/800 weights in the hero; preserve the deliberate contrast through scale, spacing, and color.

## Color

The 60/30/10 proportions describe the overall visual roles, not a pixel-count requirement: approximately 60% luminous editorial canvas across the page, 30% secondary pale surfaces plus the intentionally dark hero/feature moment, and no more than 10% accent. The hero itself is the approved dark-feature exception to the light editorial canvas.

| Role | Value | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| Dominant (60%) | `#FBFBFD` (`--color-bg-base`) | Page-wide editorial canvas outside the dark hero |
| Secondary (30%) | `#F4F4F8` (`--color-bg-subtle`); hero base `#0A1020` (`--color-dark-bg`) | Secondary light surfaces and the full-bleed dark hero field |
| Accent (10%) | `#2563EB` (`--color-accent-blue`) | Primary CTA fill, small eyebrow/rule emphasis, and visible keyboard-focus ring only |
| Destructive | `#B91C1C` (`--color-accent-tie`) | Destructive actions only; none are present in this phase |

Accent reserved for: the **Explore Projects** primary CTA, a single small status/eyebrow marker, and keyboard-focus indication. Do not color all links, discipline labels, portrait effects, or body copy blue. Use `#F0F4FF` / white for hero text over the dark overlay, and `--color-dark-text-secondary` for supporting prose. Maintain the existing restrained blue/violet ambient character only as static, low-opacity depth; no new neon palette.

### Hero Surfaces and Art Direction

- Make the portrait the dominant, full-bleed image across the hero stage; it must read as one immersive photographic scene, not a cutout card or framed portrait.
- Place the text block left-aligned over an unboxed, soft dark gradient. On desktop, use a left-to-right overlay that is strongest behind the copy (`rgba(10,16,32,0.94)` at the left, fading to near-transparent by the right half); add a gentle lower fade only as needed for the fact rail. Do not put a translucent rectangle, glass card, or opaque text panel behind the copy.
- Use `#0A1020` as the reliable background/fallback. Preserve portrait visibility and the face/upper body; set image crop/focal position deliberately and adjust it at mobile breakpoints instead of shrinking the image into a small inset.
- Use a single restrained depth layer (soft shadow, gradient, or hairline/radial detail) at a time. Keep all decorative layers `aria-hidden`, non-interactive, and behind the copy.
- Preserve the four existing verified credibility facts in a low-emphasis, unboxed rail at the hero’s lower edge: `CAF Intermediate · 5/8 Passed`; `Personal ERP & Automation Lab`; `QuickBooks Online & Xero`; `Audit & Assurance Trajectory`. Use separators on desktop and a readable 2×2 reflow on narrow screens. Do not give this rail the visual weight of a second CTA or let it overlap the portrait’s focal area.

## Desktop and Mobile Layout Contract

### Desktop (960px and wider)

- Keep the hero full-bleed, with a minimum height of `min(900px, 100svh)` and a natural content-driven height when the fact rail requires more room. Do not constrain the background image to the centered content container.
- Position the copy block at the left, vertically around the middle third, with a 64px viewport inset (or the existing `--margin-outer` at narrower desktop widths), maximum width 560px, and enough gradient coverage to maintain text contrast.
- Crop the portrait as the right/central visual anchor; reserve the face and upper body from overlay obstruction. Set the focal point explicitly (initial desktop target: around 68% horizontal) and tune against the source portrait.
- Place the CA/accounting-and-audit eyebrow before the name in reading order; render the name as the largest type. Follow with the accounting × data × AI line, concise positioning sentence, primary CTA, then quiet secondary links. Never put disciplines ahead of the CA/accounting-and-audit message.
- Keep Explore Projects as the only filled/high-emphasis action. Present CA Journey and CV as text/outline-level links, with clear spacing and no competing gradients.
- Place the factual rail at the lower edge as a non-boxed, four-column row with hairline separators. It must not float across the face or cover the primary CTA.

### Mobile and narrow viewports (below 960px; verify down to 320px)

- Keep the portrait full-bleed and visible behind the content; crop toward the face/upper body and shift the subject away from the copy as needed. Do not revert to a small portrait tile or hide the portrait.
- Use a top-to-bottom plus left-side dark gradient so the left-aligned copy and controls remain readable while the face remains visible. Do not center-align the text.
- Use 16px minimum horizontal gutters, `height: auto`, and a content-driven minimum height; allow natural vertical scrolling rather than clipping copy or controls into a fixed viewport.
- Keep the role eyebrow, h1, discipline line, and sentence in the same reading order. Let long lines wrap naturally; do not truncate or use horizontal scrolling.
- Keep the primary CTA full-width only when that improves fit; secondary CA Journey and CV links remain quieter, at least 48px high, and may wrap onto a second row. Controls must not overlap the portrait’s face or one another.
- Reflow the four fact-rail items into two columns at tablet widths and a two-by-two layout on phones. Allow labels and values to wrap without ellipsis.
- At all widths, respect safe-area insets where relevant and prevent horizontal overflow from image transforms, decorative glows, or oversized display text.

## Motion and Reduced Motion

- Use the existing Framer Motion integration only for a brief, one-time hero entrance: opacity plus at most 8px vertical translation, 350–550ms, ease-out. Keep stagger delays at or below 150ms and ensure the complete hero is readable and interactive immediately; content must not depend on animation to become visible.
- Do not add pointer-driven tilt, parallax, continuous floating, rotating rings, scroll-jacking, or always-running WebGL/canvas. Depth is primarily static CSS layering and must remain lightweight.
- Use restrained hover/focus feedback on links (color/underline or a maximum 1px lift) without layout shift. Provide equivalent visible keyboard focus; do not make hover the only feedback.
- When `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is active, render the final hero state immediately: no entrance opacity/position animation, parallax, transforms, or smooth scrolling. Keep all copy, controls, and portrait available. Retain the existing global reduced-motion rule and apply Framer Motion’s `useReducedMotion` consistently to initial as well as transition states.

## Accessibility and Interaction

- Use a semantic `<section>` labelled by its `<h1>`; use exactly one page-level h1 for the person’s name. Keep the copy order identical in the DOM and visual reading order.
- Use native anchors for `#projects`, `#journey`, and the CV download. Preserve those destinations exactly; add a concise accessible name for the download and communicate that it downloads a PDF. Do not simulate links with non-semantic clickable containers.
- The portrait is decorative when the adjacent heading identifies the person: use empty alt text (`alt=""`) so screen readers do not hear duplicate identity copy. If it conveys information not present in text, use a concise factual alternative instead.
- Keep text contrast at WCAG AA: at least 4.5:1 for normal copy and 3:1 for large text and meaningful control boundaries. Verify the gradient where every text glyph sits, not only at its leftmost stop.
- Maintain the existing visible `:focus-visible` treatment and ensure focus remains visible over both image and dark fallback. Each link/control has a minimum 48×48px target and a visible focus indicator with sufficient contrast.
- Decorative gradient, rings, arrows, and separators are hidden from assistive technology. Do not communicate status or CTA priority by color alone.
- Support keyboard activation, browser zoom/reflow, and text resizing to 200% without lost content, overlap, or horizontal scrolling.

## Asset and Performance Constraints

- Keep `/images/zohaib-portrait.png` as the canonical portrait path and preserve it as a fallback. It is currently approximately 2.1 MB; reduce transfer cost by producing appropriately sized WebP/AVIF derivatives and a responsive `srcset`/`sizes` if the existing build workflow supports them, without changing the depicted person or introducing a new remote asset.
- The hero portrait is above the fold: eager-load it, set high fetch priority, and use async decoding. Supply intrinsic width/height or an accurate aspect ratio to reserve layout space and prevent CLS. Do not lazy-load the primary hero portrait.
- Avoid layering expensive filters over the full-resolution image, large blurred surfaces, or multiple animated effects. Prefer static CSS gradients and one restrained shadow; disable nonessential effects on narrow/low-capability devices.
- Keep copy, CTA, and fallback surface visible before the image finishes loading and if it fails. No remote image/API dependency, external font fetch added by this phase, or heavyweight 3D runtime.
- Verify the hero does not cause horizontal overflow, layout shift, or a delayed primary action at mobile widths; retain the existing CSS-token and component-style architecture.
