# Requirements: Zohaib Saleem Professional Portfolio

**Defined:** 2026-10-22
**Core Value:** Help accounting and audit decision-makers quickly assess Zohaib's professional fit and credibility.

## v1 Requirements

### Professional Positioning

- [x] **IDENTITY-01**: A first-time visitor can quickly identify Zohaib's accounting/audit direction, CA-student status, and accounting × data × AI focus.
- [ ] **PROOF-01**: Visitors can scan the projects, CA journey, credentials, and capabilities that support a professional evaluation.

### Visual Design

- [x] **VISUAL-01**: The portfolio presents a cohesive, premium editorial design with clear typography, intentional spacing, and a strong information hierarchy.
- [x] **VISUAL-02**: The hero uses layered, 3D-inspired depth and cinematic visual treatment while remaining lightweight and readable.
- [x] **VISUAL-03**: Light editorial content and selective dark feature sections express the approved balanced-hybrid art direction.

### Navigation and Content

- [ ] **NAV-01**: Desktop and mobile visitors can navigate the portfolio sections and reach the primary contact action through clear, usable controls.
- [ ] **CONTENT-01**: Existing professional sections, factual claims, project assets, and valid destinations remain present and accurate through the redesign.
- [ ] **CONTACT-01**: Email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and the CV download remain discoverable and functional.

### Motion, Accessibility, and Performance

- [ ] **MOTION-01**: Entrance, hover, and scroll motion is purposeful, does not obstruct reading or interaction, and is reduced for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
- [ ] **RESP-01**: The portfolio works from narrow mobile viewports through desktop without horizontal overflow, clipped content, or unusable touch controls.
- [ ] **ACCESS-01**: Content and controls retain semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus, and readable contrast.
- [ ] **PERF-01**: Images and visual effects are optimized so the experience loads and interacts smoothly across common devices, including mobile and lower-powered devices.

## v2 Requirements

None committed. Further interactive or content features can be considered after the redesigned portfolio is evaluated.

## Out of Scope

| Feature | Reason |
|---------|--------|
| Backend services or a content-management system | This is a static professional portfolio redesign, not a data-backed product. |
| Native mobile application | Responsive web delivery covers the intended desktop and mobile audience. |
| New credentials, metrics, or work claims | The site must remain a truthful representation of the portfolio owner's experience. |
| Always-running heavyweight WebGL scene | Lightweight depth and motion are preferred to protect loading and interaction performance. |
