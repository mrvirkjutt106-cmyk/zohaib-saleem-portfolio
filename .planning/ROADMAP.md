# Roadmap: Zohaib Saleem Professional Portfolio

## Overview

This MVP redesign turns the existing React portfolio into a hiring-focused experience that helps accounting and audit decision-makers assess Zohaib's fit and credibility. It progresses from a distinctive, readable hero and visual foundation to factual professional evidence, working navigation and contact pathways, then a cross-cutting pass for accessibility, responsive behavior, motion, and performance. Work stays within the existing React, TypeScript, Vite, and Framer Motion architecture and preserves verified content and destinations.

## Phases

- [x] **Phase 1: Hiring-Focused Hero & Art Direction** - Establish the visual foundation and a clear, cinematic-but-readable first impression for accounting and audit visitors. (completed 2026-10-22)
- [ ] **Phase 2: Professional Evidence & Story** - Make verified projects, capabilities, CA journey, credentials, and direction easy to evaluate.
- [ ] **Phase 3: Navigation & Contact Pathways** - Let desktop and mobile visitors move through the portfolio and reach contact or the CV.

## Phase Details

### Phase 1: Hiring-Focused Hero & Art Direction

**Goal**: Accounting and audit visitors can immediately understand Zohaib's professional direction through a polished, balanced-hybrid opening experience.
**Depends on**: Nothing (first phase)
**Requirements**: IDENTITY-01, VISUAL-01, VISUAL-02, VISUAL-03
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. A first-time visitor can identify Zohaib's CA-student status, accounting/audit direction, and accounting × data × AI focus from the opening experience.
  2. The hero communicates depth and a cinematic character through lightweight 3D-inspired effects without obscuring its text or identity.
  3. Visitors encounter a coherent editorial visual language, combining readable light content with selective dark feature moments and clear hierarchy.
  4. The opening experience remains legible and usable at narrow mobile and desktop widths, with no clipped content or horizontal overflow.

### Phase 2: Professional Evidence & Story

**Goal**: Hiring visitors can evaluate Zohaib's fit using a clear, truthful account of his professional evidence and direction.
**Depends on**: Phase 1
**Requirements**: PROOF-01, CONTENT-01
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. A visitor can scan the projects, capabilities, CA journey, and credentials as evidence relevant to an accounting/audit opportunity.
  2. Existing professional sections, factual claims, project assets, and valid destinations remain present and accurate; no credentials, metrics, or experience are invented.
  3. Visitors can read and compare the evidence comfortably at narrow mobile and desktop widths without clipped content or horizontal scrolling.

### Phase 3: Navigation & Contact Pathways

**Goal**: Visitors can move directly to relevant portfolio content and act on a professional contact opportunity.
**Depends on**: Phase 2
**Requirements**: NAV-01, CONTACT-01
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. Desktop and mobile visitors can navigate to the portfolio's main sections and primary contact action using clear, usable controls.
  2. Email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and the CV download are discoverable and each opens its intended working destination.
  3. At narrow mobile and desktop widths, navigation remains usable, touch targets are practical, and controls or content do not clip or create horizontal overflow.

### Phase 4: Accessible, Responsive & Smooth Experience

**Goal**: Visitors can use the complete redesign comfortably across devices, input methods, and motion preferences.
**Depends on**: Phase 3
**Requirements**: MOTION-01, RESP-01, ACCESS-01, PERF-01
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. Entrance, hover, and scroll motion supports comprehension without obstructing reading or interaction, and is reduced when a visitor prefers reduced motion.
  2. Visitors can use the content and controls with semantic structure, keyboard navigation, visible focus, and readable contrast.
  3. The complete portfolio adapts from narrow mobile viewports through desktop without horizontal overflow, clipped content, or unusable touch controls.
  4. Images and visual effects load and interact smoothly on common devices, including mobile and lower-powered devices.
